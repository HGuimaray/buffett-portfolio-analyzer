Target Price Calculator

Discounted owner earnings, Berkshire Hathaway holdings

This calculator estimates a target price per share using Warren Buffett's owner earnings method: projecting a company's owner earnings forward, discounting them back to the present, and applying a margin of safety. It's scoped to companies in Berkshire Hathaway's portfolio — long-established, stable businesses — so a few inputs that would normally require deeper analysis have been fixed as constants:

  • Growth rate: 4% — a conservative stand-in for the company-specific rate you'd otherwise derive from the last 10 years of financials.
  • Margin of safety: 10% — lower than the 25% often used for less predictable businesses, reflecting the relative stability of Berkshire holdings.

Where to find these on Yahoo Finance

Go to the company's page on finance.yahoo.com, select the Financials tab, then Cash Flow Statement. Use the most recent annual (TTM or latest fiscal year) figures for:

  • Diluted average shares — found under Cash Flow > Diluted Average Shares (near the bottom of the statement).
  • Operating cash flow — listed as Operating Cash Flow (or "Cash Flow from Operating Activities").
  • CapEx — listed as Capital Expenditure (usually shown as a negative number — enter it as a positive value here).

You can enter values with a suffix (K = thousand, M = million, B = billion) or as full numbers — e.g. 5B and 5,000,000,000 are treated the same. Do not include a space between the number and the suffix (use 5B, not 5 B).
